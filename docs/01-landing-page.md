To ensure your designs look professional and consistent, here are the **8 key mistakes** discussed in the video, along with a **bonus tip**, that you should instruct your design agent to address:

1.  **Establish Clear Visual Hierarchy** Stop forcing users into rigid scanning patterns like the 'F-pattern.' Instead, use **contrast** (size, weight, color) to guide the viewer's eye through the content in a deliberate order of importance.
2.  **Maintain Component Consistency** Avoid using too many variations of the same element. Standardize your design system to include only one primary button, one secondary button, and one or two card styles. This improves both design quality and developer handoff.
3.  **Ensure Color Accessibility** Every text element must be easily readable. Use tools like a *Figma* contrast checker to guarantee that your color combinations meet accessibility standards, especially for those with visual impairments.
4.  **Apply Colors Strategically** Utilize the **60-30-10 rule** (60% neutral, 30% secondary neutral/color, 10% accent/CTA) to create balance. You can adapt this ratio depending on whether the brand identity is minimal, fun, or maximalist, but always ensure the Call-to-Action (CTA) stands out.
5.  **Implement a Type Scale** Do not use random font sizes. Define a strict hierarchy (H1, H2, H3, H4, Body 1, Body 2, Buttons, Links) and stick to it across the entire project. Use a *Figma* type scale tool to remove guesswork.
6.  **Break Up Long Text** Avoid walls of text that discourage reading. Break content into smaller paragraphs and use subheadings to make information scannable and digestible for the user.
7.  **Standardize Visual Assets** All images must share a consistent look and feel. Use a uniform color grade (same warmth, contrast, and grain/noise) on all photography so they appear to belong to the same brand identity.
8.  **Follow Full Visual Language** Do not limit brand guidelines to just fonts and colors. Incorporate the entire visual system, including corner radii, shadow styles, spacing, illustration types, and icon styles (filled vs. outline) to ensure the design feels cohesive.

**Bonus Tip: Integrate Meaningful Animations**
Elevate the experience by adding intentional animations, such as hover states, page scroll effects, or loading animations.
